AI Workflow

User input→ Prompt + item context→ AI classification→ Explanation→ Disposal guidance

In a production version, an image-capable AI model can replace the sample-item selector and identify the waste from an uploaded image. Local municipal rules should be used as the final disposal reference.

Responsible AI

  • Fairness: use varied examples and avoid assumptions from limited training examples.
  • Transparency: explain the predicted category and provide a confidence/uncertainty note.
  • Ethics: do not give misleading or harmful disposal instructions.
  • Privacy: avoid collecting personal or sensitive information; minimize image retention.
